About

Classic brand building, data-first execution.

I'm a brand and marketing professional managing the rice and lentil portfolio at Akij Resources — where I executed the 360° campaign that delivered the portfolio's highest incremental sales growth of the period, supported by ATL/BTL activation and multi-million digital reach.

My experience spans the whole journey: brand positioning and annual budget planning, national TV & digital media, packaging development, go-to-market for new launches, trade & channel activation, and influencer/UGC-first content that prioritises organic reach over paid media for cost efficiency.

I combine classic brand building with a data-first working style — including AI-assisted analysis of sales and campaign data — because I believe a brand is only as strong as the trust it earns at the moment of purchase. My work always closes the loop between what we say and what actually happens in the market.
