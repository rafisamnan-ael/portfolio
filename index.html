About

Classic brand building, data-first execution.

Brand marketing professional at Akij Resources (one of Bangladesh's largest business groups), working on the rice and lentil portfolio across 10+ product segments and 20+ consumer SKUs — from campaign delivery to go-to-market and trade activation.

I sit between planning and execution. I contribute to brand plans and media budgets, then own the delivery that brings them to life: integrated campaigns across TV, digital and social, packaging development, influencer & UGC content, and launches that reach the shelf and the consumer.

I work data-first — including AI-assisted analysis of sales and campaign performance — because a brand is only as strong as the trust it earns at the moment of purchase. My focus is closing the loop between what we communicate and what actually happens in the market.
